Card A — ArcGauge (registry 29): the large form

Where a datum is a share of a whole, the form is an arc, not a bar — the same primitive from a 26 pt table cell to a 212 pt dial. ArcGauge is the large scale: a proportion that owns real screen area, such as Coaching HQ's game-plan completion.

Track colour is white at 0.09 opacity in both appearances; the fill takes the role the datum calls for (action.primary for a neutral share, a state role where the share itself is a judgement).

Contract — reference-library plan §4, point by point

  1. Light and dark: dark shown; light appearance takes the same geometry with the light-appearance ink/fill roles from tokens-v4 Card A — no new pairing.
  2. Widths: intrinsically sized, unaffected by screen width.
  3. 44 pt floor: not applicable — a readout, not a control.
  4. States: neutral (gold) and judgement (state role) shown.
  5. Team colour: never — an arc gauge's fill is action.primary or a state role, not team colour, per §5's six-slot restraint.
  6. VoiceOver: one sentence per gauge, written out above; the printed figure is the accessible value, not the arc's sweep.
  7. Animations: in product, the arc animates to its value on appearance; Reduce Motion renders it complete immediately, since the end state is what the value already asserts.
  8. Verdict line: the gauge is evidence for a verdict line elsewhere on its screen, not a verdict itself — it prints a share, not a judgement.
  9. Density-budget cost: one dominant-object-class element; counts toward the one allowed per screen when it is the dominant object, as on Coaching HQ.
  10. Traceability: the proportion rule and the four-scale claim are quoted from 04 §6.5; the track opacity is quoted from Floodlit's shipped `Color.white.opacity(0.09)`, restated here as a specification rather than a canon colour token, since track opacity is not a §6.1 role.

Contract — reference-library plan §4, point by point

  1. Light and dark: geometry unchanged; ink/fill roles from tokens-v4.
  2. Widths: a 26 pt ring fits any dense-table column width in the six-to-nine budget.
  3. 44 pt floor: not applicable — a readout inside a row, not a tap target on its own; the row itself carries the 24–28 pt track and, where tappable, the row's own 44 pt target.
  4. States: neutral and heat-banded (positive) shown; §6.4's heat rule — red below 70, amber 70–84, green 85 upward — applies to the fill colour, never replacing the printed number.
  5. Team colour: never.
  6. VoiceOver: one sentence per ring, written out above.
  7. Animations: none at table scale — a list of these animating independently would be motion noise; Reduce Motion is therefore moot here by design, not by exemption.
  8. Verdict line: not a verdict; evidence.
  9. Density-budget cost: a fact column inside the table, one of the six to nine §4.5 allows.
  10. Traceability: the 26 pt default and "smallest size the arc still reads at" are quoted from 04 §6.5; the heat bands are quoted from §6.4.

Contract — reference-library plan §4, point by point

  1. Light and dark: track colours are the same state/action/tier roles as elsewhere; no new pairing.
  2. Widths: 212 pt fits comfortably inside the promise-floor width as the dominant object of a profile screen; reflows in place at every supported width.
  3. 44 pt floor: not applicable — a readout.
  4. States: a full six-attribute set shown; a player missing a rated attribute for his position simply omits that track rather than drawing an empty one.
  5. Team colour: never — every track is a content/state/tier role.
  6. VoiceOver: one sentence for the whole and one for the six values, written out above — read as a list, not as six separate arc traversals.
  7. Animations: each ring animates in on appearance in product; Reduce Motion renders all six complete immediately.
  8. Verdict line: the dial is evidence; Player Profile's verdict line sits above it under `04` §6.1's honest-degraded rule.
  9. Density-budget cost: the dominant object of Player Profile (18), priced at `04` §8 as one dominant object plus two secondary regions.
  10. Traceability: the 40–99 scale, 212 pt default, 7 pt stroke and 10 pt spacing are quoted from Floodlit's shipped `AttributeDial` parameters, restated as specification.

Card D — ShareBar (registry 32): the stated exception

A horizontal share, used where a comparison must sit in a row rather than a ring — rival programmes competing for the same recruit is the canonical case, because several shares must be scanned in one glance rather than read one ring at a time.

Contract — reference-library plan §4, point by point

  1. Light and dark: track white at 0.09 in both; fill takes the role assigned per row (here, a mix of action.primary, state.info and content.secondary to distinguish three rivals — team colour is not used, since this is a comparison, not identity).
  2. Widths: fluid, fills its column.
  3. 44 pt floor: not applicable — a readout.
  4. States: three shares shown side by side, the comparison case the primitive exists for.
  5. Team colour: deliberately not used here — see above.
  6. VoiceOver: one sentence per bar, written out above.
  7. Animations: in product a share bar animates to its width on data change; Reduce Motion renders the new width immediately.
  8. Verdict line: evidence, not a verdict.
  9. Density-budget cost: a comparison region, priced as one of the two secondary regions §4.5 allows.
  10. Traceability: the 4 pt default height and the stated exception to the arc-not-bar rule are quoted from 04 §6.5.

Card E — StarRating, Pennant, TimeoutMarks (registry 33–35): the drawn marks

Three marks drawn as shapes rather than SF Symbols, each with its own closed-class accounting in 04 §6.6.

StarRating (registry 33) — recruiting stars as blades

4 of 5 — always beside its printed figure

Blades, not clip-art stars — a thin triangular wedge rather than a five-point outline, which keeps the mark legible at 12 pt and avoids the generic-app signature §4.4 warns against. Capped at 1 member in the Rating-marks class (04 §6.6), used only in recruiting and draft evaluation, always printed beside the numeral it restates.

Pennant (registry 34) — the club mark

A depicted object — it keeps its values everywhere it appears, unlike a swatch

Not counted in §6.6's learned-symbol totals — a club mark is identity furniture under §5, not a vocabulary item the player learns; every programme's pennant is generated, never a chosen icon from a shared set.

TimeoutMarks (registry 35) — timeouts remaining

2 of 3 remaining — a filled mark is one in hand

Member of the Broadcast-marks class (04 §6.6), capped at 3 alongside the possession wedge and the key-moment mark; drawn only in Match Day chrome, always beside the printed count.

Symbol accounting for this sheet

  • StarRating is the sole member of the Rating-marks class, cap 1 (04 §6.6, moved from 23 to 25 total learned symbols on 2026-08-14 to admit this and TimeoutMarks).
  • TimeoutMarks joins the Broadcast-marks class, which moves from cap 2 to cap 3.
  • Pennant is not a learned symbol — generated identity furniture, excluded by the same rule that excludes team colour itself from the vocabulary a player must recall.
  • Learned-symbol spend added by this sheet: 2 (1 rating-class member, 1 broadcast-class member) — reconciled against §6.6's authoritative total, not asserted independently.

Contract — reference-library plan §4, point by point

  1. Light and dark: StarRating fill is action.primary in both; TimeoutMarks uses content.primary filled / content.quiet-bordered spent in both; Pennant carries generated team colour per §5's uniform-mark slot.
  2. Widths: all three are intrinsically sized inline marks.
  3. 44 pt floor: not applicable — none is an independent tap target; where a mark sits inside a tappable row, the row carries the floor.
  4. States: filled/unfilled shown for both StarRating and TimeoutMarks.
  5. Team colour: Pennant only, per §5.
  6. VoiceOver: one sentence per mark, written out above.
  7. Animations: none for StarRating or Pennant; a TimeoutMarks change in product marks the used slot with a brief fade, and Reduce Motion renders the new state immediately.
  8. Verdict line: none of the three is a verdict.
  9. Density-budget cost: each is a glyph, not a region — counted against §6.6's symbol totals, not against §4.5's region budget.
  10. Traceability: class membership and caps are quoted from 04 §6.6 as amended 2026-08-14; nothing on this card asserts a symbol not in that table.
