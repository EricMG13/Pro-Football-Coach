# Repetition-remake prompt set

## Production template

Create a production-ready flat-vector sports team logo for `{proposedName}`. The subject is `{subject}` and belongs to the `{family}` direction. Use one dominant silhouette, no more than six large filled regions, and exactly 2–3 flat colours: `{primaryColorHex}`, `{secondaryColorHex}`, with `#111827` reserved for a thick structural keyline. Keep a bright team colour on or near the outside edge for dark-surface separation. The mark must read clearly at 20px and remain clean at 32px and 44px. Use a transparent square canvas with generous clear padding.

Do not use text, letters, frames, badges, shields, roundels, scenery, gradients, shadows, texture, checkerboard, crowding, thin crossed details, or the prior recurring motif grammar (`{recurringMotif}`). Avoid repeated human-head, bird, tool, and fortification templates.

Construction references used for cohesion: batch-01 Otters, Fletchers, and Palisades, plus the batch-15 family calibration mark matching the target family (Sturgeon, Bristlecone, Squall, Eclipse, Luna Moth, or Geode).

## Targeted correction: Coelacanths

One coelacanth in clean side view: a single connected broad armored body, one lobed pectoral fin, and one thick three-lobed tail. No school, fragments, detached details, text, frame, gradient, or background.

## Targeted correction: Saguaros

One thick wind-bent saguaro with both massive arms visibly connected to the central trunk and one broad connected root base. No detached arms, extra cactus, text, frame, gradient, or background.

## Normalization

Final candidates were cropped and fitted to a 236px maximum silhouette inside a 256px RGBA canvas, quantized to the planned two team colours plus optional `#111827`, cleared of detached alpha specks, and flattened to no more than six large exact-colour regions. Production assets were not touched.
