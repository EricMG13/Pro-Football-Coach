   Evidence: core week/roster/recruiting/match tasks are direct, but at least 17 named destinations are host mismatches and three overlay buttons cannot generically route in-career (`01-evidence.md#structural-evidence`; `01-evidence.md#screen-by-screen-critique`).

   Justification: the primary action on Offer, Job Board, and other title-only routes is not identifiable because the promised task and action are absent, matching the zero anchor.

6. Good design is honest — Score: 0/3

7. Good design is long-lasting — Score: 1/3
   Evidence: the disciplined sports register is offset by glass, glow, grain, oversized condensed display type, and dense all-caps micro-labels (`01-evidence.md#per-principle-synthesis-facts`).

9. Good design is environmentally friendly — Score: 3/3
   Evidence: 0 initial JS bytes, 0 observed launch requests, 0 initial idle animations, 0 initial badges/modals/notifications, dark appearance, and reduced-motion suppression (`01-evidence.md#weight-and-friction-evidence`).

10. Good design is as little design as possible — Score: 1/3
    Evidence: shared primitives are disciplined, but duplicate destinations, repeated navigation layers, noncommitting gold actions, and ten dead props leave more than five removable elements (`01-evidence.md#structural-evidence`; `01-evidence.md#screen-by-screen-critique`).
    Justification: the design is not decoration-dominated, but too many elements and destinations fail to earn distinct roles.
