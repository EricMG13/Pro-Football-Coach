State fills, distinct from the state inks above — dark appearance only

Floodlit separates the colour that fills a chip or indicator from the colour that carries text — they measure differently in the dark appearance, so canon holds both. Ratios below read on page / on work / on raised. Light appearance has no separate fill form: every light-appearance ink on Card A already clears 4.5:1 against all three surfaces, so the ink is the fill — a chip, a rule and a sentence draw the same value. The split above is a consequence of the dark accents sitting between the 3:1 and 4.5:1 floors on raised, and it does not generalise to light.

The measured constraints — binding on every consumer (04 §6.1, 2026-08-14)

  • Every ink role meets 4.5:1 against all three surfaces in both appearances. The worst pairing is content.quiet on raised — 4.97 dark, 4.78 light — and it is the value the legible seam also takes, so the two floors move together.
  • The state fills are not inks. #FF3B54 measures 4.30 on raised: above the 3:1 non-text/large-text floor, below the 4.5:1 body floor. It fills chips, rules and indicators and colours large text; working prose in that role takes the ink form #FF8E9C (6.87 on raised).
  • A filled control inks with the ground, never with content.primary. Floodlit's goldInk #150F02 on the gold fill measures 12.08, on positive 11.07, on warning 10.47, on live 5.45. content.primary on those same fills measures 1.51 / 1.64 / 1.74 / 3.34 and is never used on them.
  • Body text over a lit world requires the deep panel — a contrast rule, not a taste one. The standard panel fill (white at 0.055) composited over the brightest point of the lit pitch (#37A868) resolves to #42AD70, on which content.primary measures 2.69 and content.secondary 1.42. The deep fill #08070E is specified at α ≥ 0.82, not the prototype's 0.70. At 0.82 the panel composites to #10241E over the brightest turf and to #08080F over night; content.primary / secondary / quiet measure 13.62 / 7.21 / 4.72 at the true worst case — the lamp-lit mown turf, not bare turf, which is what the superseded 0.78 figure was derived against. Rendered in full on depth-v4.
  • content.quiet is #8496AC, not Floodlit's shipped #65788F. The prototype value measures 4.37 / 3.85 / 3.32 and fails the body floor on every surface. The lift is a correction, recorded here so it is not silently reverted to match the prototype.
  • field.annotation at 3.96 on turf is a non-text indicator — the first-down rule — and clears the 3:1 floor for that use only. A label drawn on the field takes field.label #FFE196 (4.90 on turf). One gold cannot do both jobs.
  • Hairlines, named (04 §6.1). Structural rule — world.raised over work: 1.16 dark, 1.25 light, deliberately near-invisible. Legible seam — content.quiet on raised: 4.97 dark, 4.78 light. Glass edge — the 1 pt hairline tracing a panel's cut shape, a structural rule that carries no meaning and never substitutes for the legible seam.
  • Team fills against the work surfaces (re-measured against the Floodlit surfaces, 2026-08-14). dark-primary on dark work 1.35, on light work 12.45; light-primary on dark work 13.25, on light work 1.27; low-chroma on dark work 2.55, on light work 6.60. Surface law, rewritten 2026-08-14: glass at depth under one light, grain over everything and the lit world itself are the system — permitted because each is real depth or a real light source, never imitated material. Still forbidden: fake paper, leather, cork, stitching; shadow that does not encode depth order; gradient used to carry a value; more than three depth levels (world, standard panel, deep panel). At most two material panels are live per screen. Reduce Transparency flattens every panel to its opaque equivalent, drops the grain and replaces the world with world.page — every ratio on this card is measured against that flattened form, which is why it survives the setting unchanged.

The micro-label moves to the 10 pt Caption floor

Floodlit's Label3 — the tracked uppercase field label used throughout — shipped at 9 pt with 0.2 em tracking. That is below this section's 10 pt Caption floor and below 04b §8's authored-type check. It is a Caption, and its floor is 10 pt; the tracking is kept, because uppercase micro-labels are the one place tracking above −0.2 pt is correct — they are read as shapes, not words.
