The map is a second canvas, and it needed saying
GameMap is 1000 × 700 world units with regionCount = 8; MapCity carries x, y and marketSize; MapRegion carries talentDensity. Projected into 508 × 234 pt that is 0.5 pt per world unit and 134 programmes in the space. The same clustering arithmetic that governs the field governs here: no city label at full zoom, dots sized by marketSize, and only your programme, your opponent and your rivals named.
Three lenses, one map
REACH shades regions by how well you recruit there. TALENT shades by talentDensity. RIVALS drops the shading and draws the arcs. One canvas, three data joins — which is what stops the League tab needing three map screens and keeps the two-tap ceiling.
Realignment is this screen, animated once a year
02 §8's realignment changes conference membership. It does not need a screen — it needs this screen with a before and after, and a `LowerThird`-register card naming what moved. A readout that changes once a season is an event, and events get named.
Open: the map has no accessible form yet
A spatial readout is the hardest thing in the product for VoiceOver, harder than the field — the field has 22 named marks and a sentence per snap, this has 134 positions and no natural order. The likely answer is that the map is decorative and the verdict panel beside it carries the whole meaning, in which case the canvas is marked accessibility-hidden and the panel is the screen. Stated, not resolved.

WHAT THIS GROUP SENDS BACK TO 04
§3 gains MapCanvas
A second canvas with its own clustering problem, its own lens model and an unresolved accessible form. It is not a FieldCanvas variant and pretending it is would inherit the wrong rules.
§4 splits the League row
Map, standings, schedule, rankings and news are five screens, not a comma list. §4 calls itself a budget and a budget that hides four screens in one cell is not counting.
A table is a readout
§4's verdict rule already says so, but standings is the surface most likely to be built as a bare grid. Name it explicitly or it ships as wallpaper.
Realignment is an event, not a state
It happens once a season and changes the map. It wants the before/after and a named card, which means the League tab has a moment in it — and §1.5's "the game initiates" applies here too.
