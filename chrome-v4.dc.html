Selection rule unchanged (04 §6.3)

  • The current route carries boundary (the gold fill itself, which at ≥3:1 against the strip is a legible boundary in its own right), value (the bold label) and spoken state (VoiceOver adds the selected trait) — never a coloured fill alone without the other two.

Gold is the one field, everywhere else is outlined (04 §5, 2026-08-14)

  • A gold field is permitted only on the committing action. Inspect and Delegate take the ghost form — outlined, no fill — so that gold reading as "the thing that advances the game" stays true across every screen, not diluted across every button.
  • A filled control inks with the ground, never with content.primary. #150F02 (or the equivalent world.page ink) on the gold fill measures 12.08 dark; content.primary on the same fill measures 1.51 and is never used there — quoted from tokens-v4's constraint callout.
  • Delegate produces a receipt, not a state change to itself — the control is replaced by the receipt, one discrete step, no crossfade.
