Pro Football Coach — failure and interruption states, failure-v4

Supersedes failure-v3, which rendered the retired violet system. Renders 04 §6.5 registry 23: EmptyState, ErrorBanner, InterruptedState — "the failure set, inside the owning composition" — plus loading and delegated-receipt, drawn beside them because a failure state cannot be judged without its neighbours: what an interruption looks like only means something next to what running and resuming look like. This is the sheet chrome-v4 card 5 defers to for these five states, and the one this container can genuinely render and inspect.

This sheet is a rendering — 04 is the only canonical home. "Loading never displays invented percentage progress" and "Empty, error, interrupted and resume states remain inside the composition they belong to" (04 §7). Registry 23 is provisional under the P11 three-production-uses rule; these cards are the approval input, not the promotion. The InterruptedState's preserved-items line is governed by the verdict-state rule's shipping/target pattern: the shipping form ships today because G-15 (partial-advance completion record) does not exist; the target form is labelled "once G-15 lands" and is never the unlabelled default.
