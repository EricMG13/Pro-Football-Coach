The v2 critique's first P0: eleven device frames and every one of them dark. 04 §6 treats both appearances as equal, so every light-mode ratio in §2.1 was unexercised by any drawn surface. Second P1: the 932 × 430 ceiling was specified this session and never drawn. Both closed here.
The work halved once measured. See §1 — the broadcast register turns out to be appearance-independent, so only DESK screens have a light variant to draw.

And measuring it found a real defect nobody had looked for
The bug's black block against the turf is 2.03:1 — under the 3:1 non-text floor. The numerals on it are 21:1, so the content was never at risk; what is at risk is the edge of the furniture, which is the thing that tells the eye the bug is an overlay and not part of the field. Fix is one line: a rgba(255,255,255,.22) hairline on the bug's lower edge, which reads as 3.4:1 against both the block and the turf. Broadcast bugs on television have this hairline for exactly this reason and it looked like styling until it was measured.

Four things that had to change, and only four
The median tick flips from white to near-black — it was invisible on a light track. The bar track goes from #1B2130 to #DDE1E8. Elevation flips mechanism, from surface-step to shadow, per §2.3's two definitions. And the home indicator inverts. Everything else is a token swap, which is the design system working.
Light costs the app its atmosphere, and that is the honest trade
Dark is where the broadcast world lives: a green field glowing on near-black is the whole point, and this screen is competent rather than atmospheric. The team-colour header rule is what saves it — it is the only thing on the light screen that says which programme this is. Two options for the owner, both defensible: ship light as an equal appearance, or declare the product dark-only and amend §6. Drawing it was the only way to have that conversation with evidence.
Rating chips in light are already solved
The v1 ladder was verified in light and its mid-step substitution (#1F6099, 6.58:1) exists precisely for this appearance. elite 13.47, good 7.88, average 6.58 — all with white numerals. No new work; the earlier measurement paid for itself here.

This is the regular-width case
Plus and Pro Max report regular horizontal size class in landscape; the floor and Pro report compact. The match view does not care — it is one canvas. Every DESK screen does, and this reference does not yet draw a two-pane desk screen at 932. Named as the remaining hole rather than implied closed.
WHAT THIS GROUP SENDS BACK TO 04
§2.4 — the broadcast register is appearance-independent
It composites against the field, not the page. Stating it removes a whole class of duplicated work and tells ContrastByConstructionTest that broadcast pairs need measuring once, not twice.
§3 — ScoreBug needs a hairline
Its black block is 2.03:1 against turf. The numerals are safe; the furniture edge is not. One line, and it was invisible until measured.
§6 — light is now an owner decision, with evidence
Drawn, competent, and noticeably less atmospheric than dark. Ship it as equal, or declare dark-only and amend §6. Either is defensible; silence is not.
