## Design Specificity Verdict

**Visibly AI-shaped and category-interchangeable.** The composition uses a saturated dark-dashboard grammar: one large rounded hero panel, a stacked metric rail, repeated uppercase eyebrows, icon-plus-metric rows, glossy blue primary buttons, soft outlined cards and generic chevrons. Replace the football copy and helmet and the same frame could manage a racing team, esports roster or sales operation.

The deterministic scan returned zero findings because the target is a PNG and the detector has no raster/OCR engine. That is not a clean bill of health. Raster inspection instead found true-scale legibility problems, token violations, contrast failures, mixed icon styles and incorrect landscape-iPhone chrome.

No reliable browser overlay was available because the target is a standalone raster with no source page or DOM.

## Overall Impression

The verdict-first interaction has value, but the render mistakes polished dashboard furniture for product specificity. It is neither a faithful iPhone surface nor a convincing coaching workspace. The biggest opportunity is to stop presenting a recommendation dashboard and put the defensible practice choices, scarce hours and consequences directly on the field of view.

## What’s Working

1. The verdict precedes the data, which matches the product’s readout rule.
2. Evidence and delegation are first-class paths rather than afterthoughts.
3. The week has temporal shape through deadline, current work and upcoming commitments.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] It fails at actual landscape-iPhone scale

At 844×390, captions and metadata collapse toward 8–10 pt, prominent controls appear below 44 pt, safe areas are absent and the fixed two-column layout has no credible AX5 behavior. Redraw at 1:1, use the specified bottom destination bar, reserve sensor/home-indicator insets, enforce 12 pt type and 44 pt hit regions, and define single-column accessibility reduction.

Suggested command: `$impeccable adapt`

### [P1] Generic operations-dashboard grammar overwhelms product identity

Repeated rounded panels, glossy gradients, uppercase eyebrows, metric tiles and mixed sports icons are the exact reasons the result reads as slop. Remove most containers and decorative icons. Let programme identity, the named coordinator, practice schedule, film evidence, opponent personnel and historical consequences create the visual language.

Suggested command: `$impeccable quieter`

### [P1] “Practice allocation” contains no executable allocation

The interface declares a best answer, then hides the alternatives, hour costs, trade-offs and consequences behind Choose Focus. Show two to four defensible options inline, each with hours, expected benefit, downside, affected unit and uncertainty. Preselect the recommendation without committing it.

Suggested command: `$impeccable shape`

### [P1] Continue competes with and may bypass the mandatory decision

Choose Focus and Continue are both primary-blue, while Tuesday’s unresolved deadline conflicts with Wednesday “NOW.” Use one contextual primary action—Commit Practice Focus, Accept Recommendation or Delegate—and intercept Continue until mandatory work is committed or delegated. Confirm autosave and offer undo.

Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify`

### [P1] The screen outruns simulation truth

`+0.6`, `78%`, “best response” and causal pressure explanations appear authoritative even though the backend contract says these projections still require simulation. Bind every insight to a named field and evidence path; use qualitative or ranged confidence when decimals are not defensible and mark prototype values visibly until the read model exists.

Suggested command: `$impeccable harden`

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex — power user:** Cannot accept or adjust the recommendation in one tap; alternatives and opportunity cost are hidden; delegation lacks persistent rules; Continue behavior is unpredictable.

**Jordan — first-timer:** Run fit, front seven, missed fits and YPC are unexplained; “best response” contradicts “choose”; pressure and confidence scales are opaque; the largest global action invites skipping the unresolved task.

**Sam — accessibility-dependent:** Metadata falls below the 12 pt floor after rescaling; controls and chevrons appear below 44 pt; the layout cannot survive AX5 without structural reflow; focus, saved, disabled and validation states are absent.

## Cognitive Load

Four checklist failures: single focus, chunking, one thing at a time and progressive disclosure. The disclosure is inverted: opponent, pressure, health, inbox and future commitments are all visible, while the essential alternatives and trade-offs are hidden.

## Minor Observations

- The player’s own programme and coach identity are missing.
- Helmet and shield illustrations communicate less than scheme or personnel evidence.
- The raster uses glossy blue gradients instead of flat accent surfaces; sampled white-on-blue contrast is approximately 2.88:1–3.36:1, below the 4.5:1 commitment.
- Static evidence uses chartreuse-adjacent color while the actual NOW state is muddy olive, weakening Live-Is-Now.
- Icon styles vary between outline, filled, cartoon and illustrative forms.
- The pane ratio is about 66/34, not the documented 38/62 list-detail chassis.
- The commitment connector is decorative unless it encodes dependency or progress.

## Questions to Consider

- Is HQ a dashboard, or the queue for the next consequential coaching decision?
- If staff already knows the best response, where is the defensible second-best choice?
- Why is the opponent more visible than the programme the player has built?
- Is 78% genuine simulation knowledge or confidence theatre?
- What is the satisfying end-state: selecting a focus, allocating scarce hours, or committing a weekly philosophy?
