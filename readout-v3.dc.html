14 — Meter

Capacity track with a defined over-capacity state that draws past the track, plus the ratio stated as text (01-RESEARCH §6.6 §3.8: a full-but-capped bar cannot show how far over you are, so the overage is drawn beyond the 100% cap mark at its true fraction). The ledger composition follows F4's compliance-led ordering: compliance state first, composition of the total second, projection with an alarm state third, two seasons out. The header wears used-of-capacity per F19. The financial exception is honoured: this ledger carries state and ratio, never a prose judgement — 6.6 §3.1 states the pattern precisely: financial screens carry figures, direction and a compliance flag with no prose verdict, because judgement is stated only where the player must form one. Money is integer dollars throughout (CLAUDE.md).
