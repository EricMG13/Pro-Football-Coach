13 — VerdictLine

Engine-backed judgement line heading a readout: one plain-language sentence, attributed to named staff (owner decision 2026-08-12: staff-voice attribution is a requirement, not an option — gap-register resolution 9), with sample size and confidence visible, then the computation class that backs it. 04 §6.5's verdict-state rule governs this card and every rendition on this sheet. The shipping form is the verdict slot drawn empty with its gap ID, and it leads, because the backing computation G-02 (league-week baselines, percentiles, expectation deltas) does not exist and 04 §4.4 rejects invented authority — it is what a player sees today. The target form is the populated verdict, labelled "once G-02 lands"; it is never the unlabelled default and never appears at a width or AX5 rendition without the shipping form beside it. Two target instances are drawn rather than one happy one: registry 12's confidence vocabulary is three-valued, so a thin sample at low confidence — the state that makes a simulation honest under uncertainty — is drawn beside the high-confidence case. Verdict voice is Analyst Sample, the shared cast's analytics staff, synthetic — pending generator output.

14 — Meter

Capacity track with a defined over-capacity state that draws past the track, plus the ratio stated as text (01-RESEARCH §6.6 §3.8: a full-but-capped bar cannot show how far over you are, so the overage is drawn beyond the 100% cap mark at its true fraction). The ledger composition follows F4's compliance-led ordering: compliance state first, composition of the total second, projection third, two seasons out. The committed rows print to the dollar because the simulation owns them; the two projection rows are bands, because 04 §4.2 gives exact numbers only where the simulation owns exact numbers and a multi-season cap projection is an estimate by construction. The header wears used-of-capacity per F19. The financial exception is honoured: this ledger carries state and ratio, never a prose judgement — 6.6 §3.1 states the pattern precisely: financial screens carry figures, direction and a compliance flag with no prose verdict, because judgement is stated only where the player must form one. Money is integer dollars throughout (CLAUDE.md).
