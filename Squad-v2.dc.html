Every control here is a shipped enum

The screen cannot offer a plan the engine cannot run, because the controls are the engine's vocabulary rather than a parallel one.
Four segmented controls, and AX3 breaks three of them
The gap control has four segments and 04 §3's rule says a SegmentedControl becomes a list above AX3. At AX5 this screen is a single scrolling column of four lists with the commit pinned — which is the chassis rule from §4 doing its job, not a special case.

P3 shipped four unreachable enum cases and only a reachability test found them; a screen that enumerates the enum makes the same class of defect visible to a human.
The aftermath is where the engine's honesty becomes visible
SnapOutcome carries the matchups that produced it — the whole reason D2 rejected the distribution model. So the aftermath can say which protection duel lost, not just "sacked". This screen is the cheapest place to spend that, and if it ships with generic summaries then D2's model was bought and never used.
The compact ScoreBug does the header
Winner's figure in live, FINAL in the situation slot. Same component as the league readout and the career line — three surfaces, one thing, which is why it is in the registry rather than assembled per screen.
Open: does the aftermath carry the decision
§4 marks it "READOUT + occasional decision" and does not say which decisions. Injury follow-ups and discipline are the obvious candidates and both are P7/P9. Drawn as a pure readout; the decision slot is a stated hole.

WHAT THIS GROUP SENDS BACK TO 04
Controls are the engine's enums
The game plan's four axes are PlayCall's shipped cases. A view that invents a parallel vocabulary can offer a plan the engine cannot run — 08's first failure mode, in the other direction.
Enumerate the enum, draw the zeroes
The aftermath shows all nine DriveEnding cases including the ones that did not occur. A grid that hides empties cannot show that a case is never reachable.
Sparkline has two domains
Five games, and a whole career. §3 specifies only the first. Same component, different domain and axis labels — state it or a second chart appears.
One budget has no breach state
Practice hours are zero-sum, so Meter's over-capacity state is unreachable by construction. Worth naming so nobody adds it for consistency with cap and scholarships.
