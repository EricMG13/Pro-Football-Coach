Group broadcast · Registry entries 20 ScoreBug, 21 LowerThird, 22 CallInCard, plus an adopted KeyMomentsRow marks row (01-RESEARCH §6.6 §3.4). Register: BROADCAST, not DESK — radius 0 throughout (04 §6.3 "Broadcast radius: 0"), field grammar colours as the backdrop, square geometry, team identity, no management chrome (04 §2, §9). This sheet is a rendering; 04 is the only canonical home — a value appearing only here has not shipped (reference-library plan §5).

Data-source honesty. Every card draws a recorded outcome read model.
