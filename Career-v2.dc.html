WHAT THIS GROUP SENDS BACK TO 04
The promotion is a screen, not a word
§4's Career row says "the carousel is a DESTINATION" and nothing about the promotion. It is the product's headline and it needs its own row, its own §2.2 test, and a real decline path.
§2.4 pays for itself here
The two houses touching is the promotion screen's entire argument. A package system built for match furniture turns out to be the thing that makes the tier change feel like an event.
Four permanent rule colours
One per stakeholder group, used everywhere the group speaks — career, inbox, appointment. That is what makes §7's "recognisable without a name attached" achievable without portrait art.
Being fired is an inbound event
The carousel is drawn as a destination, but arriving at it is the most consequential thing the game ever initiates. §1.5 applies, and P9 owns whether it interrupts.
