THE PLACEMENT RULE
Never in the navigation bar's leading slot. That slot is where the cancellation gesture lives, and the prior build put "sim the rest of the game and commit it" there. A destructive action is a sheet action or a trailing action, always confirmed, always labelled with what will happen — never "OK", never "Continue".

WHAT THE COMPONENT GROUP SENDS BACK TO 04
The light-primary card is red-bordered because it is fiction. All 32 TeamTable pairs are dark primaries with a light secondary, so the range the brief asks these components to survive does not exist in the data. Both real pairs pass their floors comfortably. That is not reassurance — it is the observation that the floors have never been tested against the case that would break them, and 04 §2.1's generation-time contrast gate has no generator to run in.
Three components needed a rule that 04 §3 does not yet state. OpposedBar: colour encodes who is the outlier, not which side is ours, so both fills go neutral when neither is. SegmentedControl: becomes a list above AX3, because three labels at 53 pt do not fit 769 pt. LowerThird: drops its context line at AX5 rather than shrinking, since a three-line card at 53 pt would cover the marks it describes. Each is a stated reduction, which is what the no-clipping clause requires instead of a fixed-height container.
