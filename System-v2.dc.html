PRO FOOTBALL COACH · DESIGN REFERENCE v2 · GROUP: SYSTEM
Waiting, confirming, settings, symbols
Four gaps the critique named. The product has one moment where the player waits on the simulation and it was undrawn; the most dangerous control in the app had its confirmation described in prose; settings was a button; and every glyph in ten reference files is a geometric placeholder.
SF Symbol names below are proposals, not verified. The catalogue was not reachable from this environment. Confirm each exists at the stated weight before P11.

Determinate, because the engine knows the denominator
134 programmes and 32 pro teams are constants in 02 §11. A spinner would be a lie about knowable work. An indeterminate spinner is the correct shape only when the total is unknown, and here it never is.
Results arrive during the wait, not after it
Scores land as they resolve, newest in live. Two seconds of watching the league play is content; two seconds of a progress bar is a loading screen. This is also the cheapest place in the product to make the off-screen model feel like a world rather than a batch job — D3 and D4 built it and nothing displayed it.
If it finishes in 300 ms, do not show this
2.0 s is the ceiling, not the expectation. Below ~400 ms this screen is a flash and worse than nothing. Rule: show after 400 ms, hold a minimum of 600 ms once shown. Otherwise the fastest device gets the ugliest transition, which is the standard way this pattern fails.
2  CONFIRMATION · THE MOST DANGEROUS CONTROL IN THE PRODUCT
End the Ashgrove career?
Year 3, week 9, 6–2. Two conference titles. 41 seasons of record book.
There is one save. This cannot be undone.
Three rules this render establishes. The destructive action names what it destroys with real numbers, not "your progress". The safe action is first and visually heavier, reversing the usual dialog order because the default here should be retreat. And the destructive button is outlined rather than filled — a filled red button reads as the primary action, and it is not.

THE RULE, AND THE RISK
SF Symbols only, no custom glyph art, per 04b's Platform Conformance dimension and AUDIT.md:500. A bundled icon set would be a maintenance surface and an accessibility one — SF Symbols scale with Dynamic Type and carry accessibility labels for free.
Every name above is a proposal. The catalogue was not reachable from this environment, so each needs confirming for existence and for availability at the iOS 17 deployment target. binoculars.fill and gavel.fill are the two most likely to be wrong.
