The week is the screen when time is the task (T6: chronology is the spine, and time is the natural compression). Seven day columns, each with a stated capacity of three typed session slots; fixtures, travel and rest occupy the same grid cells as practice sessions, so a congested week visibly spends preparation (F36 — the fixture list eats the plan, in place). The collection wears its bound at the header (F39 kin, 21 slots); the fixture wears its distance ("tomorrow", F30); an empty day states its own absence in place (F33); the arithmetic footer prints what the week spent. Scenario: a short week — the fixture fell on Thursday, so Wednesday holds travel and the game consumes its day.
