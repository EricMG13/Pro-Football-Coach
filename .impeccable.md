## Design Context

### Users

The primary audience is experienced mobile management-sim players who have run out of football games they trust over a long career. A secondary audience is desktop football-sim players who want a credible coaching career for a commute. They make consequential coaching, roster, recruiting, staff, scheme, match-call, draft, cap, and career decisions; they do not directly control players.

### Brand Personality

Credible, consequential, broadcast-authentic. The product should feel like a durable fictional football world whose decisions have visible costs, not a generic productivity dashboard or an arcade game.

### Aesthetic Direction

Dark is native and light is equal. BROADCAST surfaces use team-filled, square, tabular, on-field geometry; DESK surfaces use neutral measured surfaces, semantic team accents, and restrained depth. Current activity receives a narrow live emphasis. Generated teams, programmes, colours, people, and broadcast identities remain wholly fictional and original. Do not imitate Football Manager's trade dress or owner-supplied competitor imagery.

### Design Principles

1. State the consequence before asking for a commitment; distinguish destinations from readouts.
2. Preserve the BROADCAST/DESK register split and use geometry before colour to communicate football context.
3. Design the 844 × 390 AX5 case first: reflow and author explicit reductions while keeping primary actions reachable.
4. Use only canonical semantic colour, type, spacing, radius, and elevation roles; current-activity emphasis never decorates static state.
5. Make light appearance, compact/regular width, colour-vision deficiency, reduced motion, and semantic twins equal parts of the evidence.
