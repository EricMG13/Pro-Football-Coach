PRO FOOTBALL COACH · DESIGN REFERENCE v2 · GROUP: BROADCAST PACKAGES
The occasion has a look
Owner direction: pro, college, playoffs and championships should look different from one another, the way two networks covering the same sport look different. That is right, and it solves a problem the review raised separately — that after promotion the app looked identical, so the tier change was a label rather than an event.
No in-fiction network is named. A broadcaster name is a fresh trade-mark collision surface for no mechanical gain, so the houses are named for the tier they cover. If branded networks are ever wanted they go to counsel first.
1  Two axes, not seven packages
the lazy version that covers every case in the calendar
02 §11 gives seven distinct broadcast occasions across the two tiers. Seven bespoke packages is seven things to maintain and seven ways to drift. Instead: a house (which tier is playing) crossed with an escalation (what is at stake), plus one modifier. Two houses × three escalations covers all seven occasions from about ten token values, and a new occasion is a mapping rather than a design.
AXIS 1 — HOUSE · WHO IS PLAYING
COLLEGE HOUSE
Warm. Furniture ends are cut at 9°, carrying the pennant geometry the LowerThird already uses. Frame accent bronze #D9A441. Numerals track slightly open. Occasion tags are set in small caps with wide letterspacing — the register of a programme that has done this since 1898.
PRO HOUSE
Cool. Furniture is orthogonal — every edge square, no skew anywhere. Frame accent steel #7FB2E5. Numerals track tight. Occasion tags are set as solid blocks. The register of a television product, not an institution.
Measured, and it confirms the design. Bronze #D9A441 and steel #7FB2E5 sit at 1.01:1 against each other — near-identical luminance. In greyscale, or to a monochromat, the two house accents are the same colour. That is survivable only because colour is the secondary channel here and geometry is the primary one. Had the houses been distinguished by hue alone this system would fail the never-only-colour rule outright.
The skew is the tell. One geometric rule — 9° or 0° — separates the two houses at a glance, survives at any size, costs nothing to implement, and is legible in a screenshot without a single word. It is doing the work a logo would do, without inventing a logo.

Escalation is additive and monotonic. Each tier adds furniture and never rearranges it, so a player who has learned the regular-season bug can read the championship bug instantly — the score, clock and situation are in the same places, with more around them. A package that moved things at the biggest moment would be at its least legible when it mattered most.
Modifier — rivalry. Applies to regular-season games only, where it has something to say. Adds a tag and a paired seam in the two programmes' colours. 02 §11.3 carries 8 rivalries per programme and P2 already seeds them; this is the first surface that spends one.

The top rule splits into both programmes' secondaries. The tag carries the rivalry's generated name and its meeting count — P2 already generates both and nothing has ever displayed them. This is the cheapest possible payoff for a whole generation subsystem: one rule, one string, and a regular-season week suddenly has a week that matters in it.
WHAT THIS GROUP SENDS BACK TO 04
§2 gains broadcast.*
A fourth token group beside colour, type and spacing: house geometry, house accent, three escalation heights, frame treatment. Ten values, and the contrast suite enumerates the two accents like any other colour.
The mapping is a rules constant
Occasion to package belongs in the per-tier rules module with every other constant, never inlined in a view. CLAUDE.md's no-magic-number rule already covers it.
§5 gains a package parameter
The match view is drawn once and takes a package. §5.2's arithmetic is unaffected at Regular and loses 4 and 8 pt of field height at Elimination and Final — within the 20 pt clearance, but it must be stated, not discovered.
The promotion arc now has a payoff
Crossing to the pro tier changes the house: skew to square, bronze to steel. 02 §9 called the arc a retention device; a visible change of register is what makes it feel like one.
