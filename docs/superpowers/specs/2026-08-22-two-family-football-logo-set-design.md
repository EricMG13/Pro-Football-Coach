# Two-Family Football Logo Set Design

## Objective

Create 20 new football athletics marks: ten Equipment & Vehicle subjects and ten Regional Symbol subjects. Every subject is new relative to the completed batches. The supplied reference image controls the visual language only; none of its depicted subjects will be copied.

The deliverable contains exactly 20 individual logo PNGs. Contact sheets and QA images are auxiliary review artifacts and do not count toward the 20.

## Visual Direction

The marks will use the approved current style:

- compact, asymmetric helmet-decal silhouettes;
- one dominant dark outer mass;
- two or three flat visible colours;
- broad interior colour blocks and deliberate negative-space cuts;
- hard edges, angular turns, and sharp directional points;
- no text, letters, numbers, wordmarks, mockups, or secondary objects;
- no gradients, shading, texture, lighting, depth, bevels, or sketch detail.

Every mark must look like a college or professional American-football identity at helmet, midfield, uniform, broadcast-scorebug, and mobile sizes. The darker colour carries the outside silhouette.

## Family A: Equipment & Vehicles

Each mark depicts only the named object.

1. **Locomotive** — forward three-quarter boiler wedge with one broad stack and cowcatcher cut.
2. **Biplane** — banking nose-first silhouette with two large wing bars and one tail cut.
3. **Submarine** — long side-profile hull with one conning-tower block and stern notch.
4. **Bulldozer** — low side profile led by one oversized blade and one continuous track mass.
5. **Snowplow** — frontal three-quarter truck mass dominated by a wide angular plow blade.
6. **Tugboat** — compact rising bow with one cabin block and one stack.
7. **Chariot** — forward-leaning body and one oversized wheel; no horse or driver.
8. **Cannon** — low side profile with one large barrel and one wheel mass.
9. **Fire Engine** — blunt forward cab and grille silhouette with one simplified equipment block.
10. **Steamroller** — one oversized front drum joined to a compact angular cab.

## Family B: Regional Symbols

Each mark depicts only the named natural feature.

1. **Volcano** — steep asymmetric cone with one broad crater and lava cut.
2. **Canyon** — two interlocking cliff masses defining one wide central gorge.
3. **Mesa** — stepped caprock silhouette with one deep undercut.
4. **Glacier** — forward-moving ice tongue with two large crevasse cuts.
5. **Waterfall** — hard cliff lip and one broad descending water mass.
6. **Geyser** — one forceful upward burst anchored by a low geometric base.
7. **Sand Dune** — sweeping crescent ridge with one large shadow cut.
8. **Fjord** — opposing cliff faces creating one deep descending inlet.
9. **Sea Cliff** — one sheared rock face with a broad wave-shaped negative cut.
10. **Badlands** — three joined eroded spires forming one compact skyline mass.

## Production Rules

Each logo will be generated independently so subjects do not inherit accidental motifs from one another. The reference image is a style reference, not an edit target.

Final files must satisfy all established house rules:

- exactly 256 x 256 PNG, 8-bit RGBA;
- genuinely transparent background and transparent pixels on all four edges;
- one centred subject with at least a four-percent safe area;
- two or three flat visible colours;
- maximum six connected filled regions;
- heavy, even dark keyline measuring two to 2.5 percent of the canvas;
- no retained feature or gap smaller than five percent of the canvas;
- clear recognition at a 20-point display size.

Colour choices are unrestricted by team-colour conventions. Palettes will vary across the set while retaining sufficient contrast between the outer silhouette and internal shapes.

## Validation and Rejection Rules

Each generated candidate will be rejected or simplified if it contains an extra object, resembles clip art or a corporate icon, repeats a completed subject, uses tonal effects, breaks edge transparency, exceeds the colour or region limits, or loses its identity at 20 points.

The final audit will verify dimensions, RGBA mode, binary alpha, transparent edges, safe-area margins, visible-colour count, connected-region count, and minimum retained-feature size. A 20-point review sheet and a 1024-pixel-wide phone contact sheet will provide visual verification.
