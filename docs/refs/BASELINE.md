## What is still red, and why it does not gate this work

`--team-logo-manifest` stays red on 52 mark briefs. A brief is art-direction prose, not
an identity, and it does not touch the stableID → name → world join these frames depend
on. **Do not quote "the team-logo gate is green" as a merge condition until those 52 are
fixed** — say "green except the 52 briefs" or fix them first.

## The trap this pin sets, and how the generator avoids it

`9e50b57` **decoupled the asset key from the stableID**. Before it, `TeamLogo_<hex>` was
the team's own UUID with the hyphens removed; after it, the file named
`TeamLogo_0213C958…` belongs to team `073A0F70-…`, and the hex in the filename is a
fossil of the team it was drawn for.

Slicing a stableID back out of an asset filename therefore resolves 11 of the 13 vendored
marks to the wrong team and the other 2 to none. `marks.asset_owners()` reads the
catalogue's unconditional `assetNames` map instead. Do not "simplify" it back.
